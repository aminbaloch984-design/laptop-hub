## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
NEW AI is a personal AI agent for your phone. It talks like a normal assistant, remembers
what you tell it, and can use a small set of browser tools when you give permission.

CHAT
Ask anything: code, research, writing, maths, planning or translation. Answers stream in as
they are written, so you can stop a long reply the moment it stops being useful. Code blocks
are formatted and copyable.

VOICE
Tap the microphone and talk instead of typing. Optional spoken replies read the answer back,
and a hands-free wake phrase lets you start a conversation without touching the screen.
Speech recognition uses the engine built into your device.

MEMORY
Tell NEW AI a preference once, such as "I prefer concise answers" or "I write in JavaScript".
Only what you approve is stored, it stays on your device, and you can edit or delete every
entry at any time.

AGENTS
Describe a goal and NEW AI breaks it into steps, runs them one by one and reports progress.
Anything sensitive waits for your approval before it happens.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
- No account and no sign-up.
- Chats, memories and settings are stored on your device, not on our servers.
- No advertising and no third-party analytics in this version.
- Your messages are sent to the AI provider only to produce the answer you asked for.

HONEST LIMITS
NEW AI cannot control your phone's files or other apps, and it can be wrong. Verify important
information before you rely on it.

Built by Nabeel Ameen.

Privacy policy: https://aminbaloch984-design.github.io/nabeel-ameen/new-ai/privacy.html
```
